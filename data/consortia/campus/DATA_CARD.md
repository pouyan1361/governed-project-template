# Data card: Nazarian Advising Consortium (4 academic departments)

Four departments of one college want an early-alert model for students at risk of failing a core course. FERPA and department autonomy keep student records inside each department.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (failed_course)**: 1 if the student earned below a C or withdrew after the drop deadline

**Unit of analysis**: one student-course enrollment. **Members**: 4. **Features**: 12 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: course_level, modality, month (1 to 12).

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Prioritize advising outreach and tutoring referrals in the first month.

Prohibited: Admissions, financial aid or grading decisions, sharing records across departments, labeling students to instructors.

## Prescriptive actions to govern

- send_advising_outreach (approval required)
- refer_to_tutoring (logged)
- notify_instructor (approval required)
- drop_student_from_course (forbidden for any agent)
