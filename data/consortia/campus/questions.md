# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** Where are the failures: which courses, levels and modalities in each department?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why did Marketing's failure rate rise this year, and is it the students or the gateway course?
3. **Predictive.** Which enrolled students are at risk by week four, and does Real Estate gain from the federation?
4. **Prescriptive.** Whom should advisors contact this week, and when must a human approve the outreach?
5. **Cross-lens.** Working hours predict failure everywhere except Real Estate. What does the charter owe that department?
