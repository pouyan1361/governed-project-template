# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** Which industries and products carry the alliance's defaults, bank by bank?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why is High Desert's default rate up this year: borrowers, the drought, or underwriting?
3. **Predictive.** Which active loans are likely to default in 12 months, and how much does Coast Range gain from the federation?
4. **Prescriptive.** Which borrowers should be offered a restructure before they default, and who approves that?
5. **Cross-lens.** Seasonal businesses look risky everywhere except Metro Pacific. One model or two?
