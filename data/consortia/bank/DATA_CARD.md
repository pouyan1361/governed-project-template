# Data card: Cascade Community Banks Credit Consortium (5 branches)

Five community banks in a shared-services alliance want one small-business loan default model. Each bank's customer data is its own; the alliance agreement forbids pooling.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (default_12m)**: 1 if the loan went 90 or more days delinquent within 12 months

**Unit of analysis**: one small-business loan. **Members**: 5. **Features**: 12 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: industry, product, month (1 to 12).

## Members

| Member | Rows | Base rate | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cascade First | 2,400 | 0.23 | Largest alliance member |
| Willamette Savings | 1,200 | 0.31 | Serves startups and immigrant-owned businesses |
| High Desert Bank | 800 | 0.28 | Agricultural region in a drought year |
| Coast Range Credit | 70 | 0.30 | Tiny bank; cannot train alone |

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Flag applications for a second look and set monitoring intensity on existing loans.

Prohibited: Automatic decline, pricing, any use of protected characteristics, marketing.

## Prescriptive actions to govern

- escalate_to_credit_committee (approval required)
- increase_monitoring (logged)
- offer_restructure (approval required)
- decline_application (forbidden for any agent)

## Notes for the federation

Members cannot pool rows. The coordinator may hold neutral_eval.csv (2,000 consented, de-identified rows tagged by source member) for evaluation only. Standardize with the means and sds in this card, agreed in advance, so nobody shares raw statistics.
