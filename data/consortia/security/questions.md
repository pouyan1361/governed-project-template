# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** What is each tenant's alert volume, source mix and true-incident rate by month?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why is Kestrel Capital's incident rate up: more attacks, or noisier sensors?
3. **Predictive.** Which of tonight's open alerts are true incidents, and does Summit gain from the federation?
4. **Prescriptive.** Which hosts should be isolated now, and who approves that at 3 AM?
5. **Cross-lens.** Off-hours activity is a signal everywhere except Summit. Alert text is attacker-controlled: what does that mean for your agent?
