# Data card: Granite MSSP Alert Triage Consortium (4 enterprise tenants)

A managed security provider watches alerts for four client companies. Each tenant's logs are confidential to that tenant; the provider wants one true-incident model trained without moving logs between tenants.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (true_incident)**: 1 if an analyst confirmed the alert as a real incident

**Unit of analysis**: one security alert. **Members**: 4. **Features**: 12 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: alert_source, asset_tier, month (1 to 12).

## Members

| Member | Rows | Base rate | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orion Logistics | 3,200 | 0.25 | Largest tenant |

| Kestrel Capital | 1,000 | 0.32 | Targeted by a phishing campaign this quarter |
| Summit Municipal | 150 | 0.25 | Small city with night-shift public works, so off-hours activity is normal |

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Rank alerts for analysts and recommend containment steps.

Prohibited: Disabling accounts or wiping hosts automatically, sharing one tenant's indicators with another without consent, HR action against users.

## Prescriptive actions to govern

- escalate_to_analyst (logged)
- isolate_host (approval required)
- block_external_ip (approval required)
- disable_user_account (forbidden for any agent)

## Notes for the federation

Members cannot pool rows. The coordinator may hold neutral_eval.csv (1,600 consented, de-identified rows tagged by source member) for evaluation only. Standardize with the means and sds in this card, agreed in advance, so nobody shares raw statistics.
