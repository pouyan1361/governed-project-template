# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** Which ward and payer mix drives each hospital's readmissions this quarter?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why is St. Brigid's readmission rate higher than Lakeshore General's at the same age and comorbidity?
3. **Predictive.** Which of this week's discharges at Pinecrest are high risk, and does the federated model beat Pinecrest's own 90-row model?
4. **Prescriptive.** Whom should the nurses call first on Monday, and which calls need a clinician's approval?
5. **Cross-lens.** Harbor's elderly patients who live alone look safer than elsewhere. Is that real, and should Harbor get its own model?
