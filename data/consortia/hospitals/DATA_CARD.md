# Data card: Lakeshore Readmission Consortium (5 hospitals)

Five hospitals in one state want a shared 30-day readmission model but cannot pool patient records. This is the course scenario, extended: more hospitals, a new population mix, and new features. Teams must go beyond the Week 6 repo.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (readmitted_30d)**: 1 if the patient was readmitted within 30 days of discharge

**Unit of analysis**: one discharge. **Members**: 5. **Features**: 14 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: ward, payer, month (1 to 12).

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Prioritize nurse follow-up calls and discharge planning resources.

Prohibited: Coverage decisions, pricing, denying or delaying a discharge, any use by a payer.
