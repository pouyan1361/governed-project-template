# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** How did claim volume, severity and fraud rate move by region and claim type over the year?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why does South's fraud rate exceed West's, and is it the claims or the claimants?
3. **Predictive.** Which open claims this week are likely fraudulent, and how much does Coastal gain from the federation?
4. **Prescriptive.** Which claims should be held for review today, and which should be fast tracked?
5. **Cross-lens.** Late-night incidents look suspicious everywhere except Coastal. What should the charter say about Coastal?
