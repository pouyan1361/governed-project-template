# Data card: Sierra Mutual Claims Fraud Consortium (4 regional offices)

Sierra Mutual's four regional claims offices each keep their own claims. Regulators forbid moving claimant data across state lines without consent, so a fraud model must be trained federated.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (fraud_flag)**: 1 if the claim was confirmed fraudulent after investigation

**Unit of analysis**: one auto claim. **Members**: 4. **Features**: 12 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: claim_type, channel, month (1 to 12).

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Route suspicious claims to a human investigator; speed up clearly legitimate claims.

Prohibited: Automatic denial, pricing or renewal decisions, sharing claimant data across offices.

## Prescriptive actions to govern

- hold_claim_for_review (approval required)
- fast_track_payment (approval required above a limit)
- request_documents (logged)
- deny_claim (forbidden for any agent)

## Notes for the federation

Members cannot pool rows. The coordinator may hold neutral_eval.csv (1,600 consented, de-identified rows tagged by source member) for evaluation only. Standardize with the means and sds in this card, agreed in advance, so nobody shares raw statistics.
