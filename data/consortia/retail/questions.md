# Starter business questions

1. **Descriptive.** Which categories and tiers stock out most, store by store, and in which months?
2. **Diagnostic.** Why does Portland stock out more than Bellevue on the same SKUs: demand, deliveries or ordering habits?
3. **Predictive.** Which SKUs will stock out next week at Astoria, and how much does the new store gain from the federation?
4. **Prescriptive.** What should each store reorder this week, and which orders need the owner's approval?
5. **Cross-lens.** Rain predicts stockouts at Tahoe but not elsewhere. Should Tahoe fine-tune or leave?
