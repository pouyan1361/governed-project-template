# Data card: Pacific Peak Outdoor Stores Replenishment Consortium (6 franchise stores)

Six independently owned franchise stores of one outdoor brand want a shared stockout model. Franchise contracts keep each store's sales data private from the others and from the franchisor beyond summaries.

Everything is synthetic, generated by the instructor from a fixed seed. Nothing here is a real person or record.

**Outcome (stockout_next_week)**: 1 if the SKU ran out of stock at that store in the following week

**Unit of analysis**: one SKU-week at one store. **Members**: 6. **Features**: 12 in human units (see below). **Descriptive columns**: category, price_tier, month (1 to 12).

## Intended and prohibited uses

Intended: Prioritize replenishment orders and promo planning per store.

Prohibited: Sharing one store's sales with another store, pricing coordination between stores, staffing decisions.

## Prescriptive actions to govern

- place_reorder (approval required above a dollar limit)
- transfer_stock_between_stores (approval required by both stores)
- flag_for_buyer (logged)
- change_price (forbidden for any agent)

## Notes for the federation

Members cannot pool rows. The coordinator may hold neutral_eval.csv (2,400 consented, de-identified rows tagged by source member) for evaluation only. Standardize with the means and sds in this card, agreed in advance, so nobody shares raw statistics.
